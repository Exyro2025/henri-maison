# Henri — Maître de Maison

> *À votre service, Madame Australia.*

Henri is a private, PIN-protected AI butler — a Progressive Web App designed to live on your phone like a native app. He knows who you are, remembers what matters, and handles the things a world-class butler handles.

---

## What Henri does

**Entertaining & dining**
Dinner menus, wine pairings, flower arrangements by season, gift suggestions, table setting, seating, how the evening flows.

**Correspondence & occasions**
Letters, emails, invitations, thank-you notes, birthday greetings, condolences — personal, never generic.

**Wardrobe**
What to wear for any occasion. Board meetings, galas, dinner parties, travel. Specific, confident, considered.

**Social coaching & eloquence**
What to say in any room. Introductions, difficult conversations, formal occasions, networking. She walks in ready.

**Morning briefing**
Henri greets you by name, reads the time of day and your location, and sets the tone for the day.

**Household & travel**
Errands, logistics, itineraries, packing, local context. Henri prepares — you decide.

---

## Privacy & security

- **PIN-protected** — 6-digit PIN locks the app on every close
- **Stored locally** — your profile, preferences, and everything Henri learns lives only on your device
- **No server** — nothing is sent anywhere except your conversation with the AI
- **Yours alone** — no accounts, no tracking, no data sharing

---

## Your profile

Henri holds a private profile containing:
- Your identity and businesses
- Wardrobe preferences by occasion
- Flower, wine, and food preferences
- Anything he must never forget
- A growing list of things he has learned from your conversations

Access it anytime via the 👤 icon.

## Technology

- Pure HTML, CSS, JavaScript — no framework, no dependencies
- Progressive Web App (PWA) — installable on any phone via Chrome or Edge
- Powered by Claude (Anthropic) via API
- Location-aware via browser geolocation
- All data stored in localStorage on your device

## Installation

### Deploy to GitHub Pages

1. Fork or upload this repository to your GitHub account
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**
3. Set source to **Deploy from a branch → main → / (root)**
4. Save — GitHub will provide a URL within a minute

### Install on your phone

1. Open the GitHub Pages URL in **Chrome or Edge** on your phone
2. Tap the three-dot menu **⋮**
3. Select **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install App"**
4. Henri appears on your home screen with his gold crest

### First launch

On first open, Henri will ask you to:
1. Set a 6-digit PIN
2. Enter your Anthropic API key (get yours at [console.anthropic.com](https://console.anthropic.com))

That's all. Henri is engaged.
